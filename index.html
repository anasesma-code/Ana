About Adam

Not your typical therapist.

Born and bred in multicultural south London, Adam has been on his own journey of personal development, and it shows in how he understands all different types of people.

Have you been made to feel different? Have you felt judged? Is life with your thoughts and feelings difficult? Have you suffered physical or emotional abuse? Adam offers a space to get these things out of your head, see them for what they really are, and then prioritise them.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to have a "big" problem to start?

No. Every person can benefit from talking therapy. There's no taboo in speaking about your emotions, fears, confusions, anxieties or trauma, big or small.

How often should I come?

To get the most out of counselling, the recommendation is one session per week. You can pay as you go or book a block of 6 or 12 sessions.

Do you work with non-traditional relationships?

Yes. Adam works with couples, multiple lovers, families and individuals, without judgement.

How long is a couples session?

The first relationship session is 90 minutes (£120). After that, sessions are one hour at £100.

Are you properly accredited?

Adam is a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (MBACP, no. 378572), a PSA accredited register, and is listed on Counselling Directory.
